THESIS: A dark working ledger for one day. The surface refuses the category default of gradient-glass dashboard cards, emoji, hero metrics and pill controls; the day is read as a ruled sheet with a single progress line.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite ground `#0b0c0d`, flat panels `#121315`, hairlines `#26272a`, bone text `#e9e7e1`, muted `#a09e97`; no chromatic accent. Square geometry, 1px rules, system grotesque for language, mono only for dates, counts and measurements. Density from Linear, calm from Things.

STORY: The visitor opens today, understands the state in one glance (date, done/total, remaining), adds or completes tasks in one tap, then checks the week's record on the dashboard. Nothing interrupts the list.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail navigation (wordmark, Задачи, Дашборд, account). Main column: "Задачи" heading, mono date line and day controls, a 1px progress rule with tabular count, a flat add row, then the ledger list — numbered rows separated by hairlines, square checkbox, actions revealed on hover but always reachable on touch. On wide screens a narrow activity rail sits at the right.
